About Koppadi Veera Manikanta

Koppadi Veera Manikanta is an Indian entrepreneur and technology enthusiast who founded Ruchigo with the goal of building technology-driven solutions for local commerce and delivery.

He completed his B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence in 2025. His interests in software development, artificial intelligence, digital platforms and entrepreneurship contributed to the development of Ruchigo.

As Founder & CEO, he is involved in the product vision, technology development, delivery systems, restaurant partnerships, customer experience and overall development of the Ruchigo platform.

Founding Ruchigo

Ruchigo was founded in 2025 with the objective of making digital ordering and delivery services more accessible to customers and local businesses.

The idea focuses particularly on local markets and smaller towns, where restaurants and businesses can benefit from technology that helps them connect directly with customers.

Ruchigo brings together restaurant discovery, digital menus, ordering, online payments, location-based delivery, delivery partner management, customer accounts, offers, notifications and order tracking through one platform.

Founder’s Vision

“To build technology that helps local businesses reach more customers while making ordering and delivery simple, affordable and accessible for communities beyond major cities.”

Building Ruchigo Through Technology

The development of Ruchigo focuses on using technology to solve practical challenges in ordering, restaurant management and last-mile delivery.

The platform continues to evolve with systems for location-based delivery, automated delivery assignment, restaurant management, customer notifications, digital payments, offers and real-time order management.

Mission

The mission of Ruchigo is to connect customers, restaurants, local businesses and delivery partners through a simple technology platform designed around the needs of local communities.
