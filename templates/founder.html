About the Founder

KOPPADI VEERA MANIKANTA is the founder of Ruchigo, an innovative online food delivery platform created to help local restaurants reach customers faster and smarter. Inspired by successful companies like Swiggy and Zomato, the goal of Ruchigo is to bring the same level of service to small towns and new cities.

Vision

To build India's most trusted food delivery platform for small towns, giving opportunities to local restaurants and providing customers with fast, affordable, and reliable delivery.
