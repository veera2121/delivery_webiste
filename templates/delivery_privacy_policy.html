Delivery Partner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how RucHiGO handles information when delivery partners use the RucHiGO Delivery Partner application.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features used, we may process delivery partner names, phone numbers, account identifiers, assigned order information, delivery status, location information, device information and notification tokens.

2. How we use information

Information is used to authenticate accounts, assign and manage deliveries, coordinate with customers and restaurants, track deliveries, send order notifications, provide support and protect the service.

3. Location information

With the relevant device permissions, the app may collect or transmit location information during delivery-related activities, including when the app is in the background if that functionality is enabled. Location is used for rider availability, delivery coordination and tracking. You can manage location permissions in your device settings.

4. Notifications

The app may use Firebase Cloud Messaging or other configured services to send delivery assignments, order updates and operational alerts.

5. Sharing information

Relevant information may be shared with authorised RucHiGO staff, restaurants, customers and service providers when necessary to operate and complete deliveries, provide support, protect the service or comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

6. Storage and security

We apply reasonable security measures and retain information only for as long as necessary for operational, security and legal purposes. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and requests

You can manage device permissions and contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and operational retention requirements.

8. Children's privacy

The delivery partner app is intended for eligible delivery partners and is not designed for children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
