Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

RucHiGo ("RucHiGo", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the RucHiGo website, mobile application, and related services.

By using RucHiGo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, place an order, contact us, or use our services.

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address, where provided
  • Delivery address
  • Location information when you choose to share it
  • Order and transaction information
  • Account and login information

Location Information

RucHiGo may request access to your device location to help identify restaurants and delivery services available near you, calculate delivery distances and charges, provide delivery services, and improve location-based features.

Location access is used only for purposes related to the RucHiGo services. You can control location permissions through your device settings.

Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information when you use our website or application, including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Push notification/device tokens
  • Log and diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Processing and delivering food orders
  • Connecting customers with restaurants and delivery partners
  • Calculating delivery charges and delivery distances
  • Providing order status and delivery tracking
  • Sending order-related notifications
  • Sending important service communications
  • Providing customer support
  • Preventing fraud and unauthorized activity
  • Improving our website, application, and services
  • Maintaining security and system reliability
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations

3. Orders and Payments

When you place an order through RucHiGo, we collect information necessary to process and fulfill your order, including order details, delivery information, and payment-related information.

Online payments may be processed through third-party payment service providers. RucHiGo may not directly store your complete card, UPI, or banking credentials when payment processing is handled by a third-party payment provider.

Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Push Notifications

RucHiGo may use push notification services to send information such as order updates, delivery updates, important account notifications, and promotional communications.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share necessary information with trusted parties when required to provide our services, including:

  • Restaurants, to prepare and fulfill your order
  • Delivery partners, to deliver your order
  • Payment service providers, to process payments
  • Technology and infrastructure service providers
  • Analytics, security, and notification service providers
  • Government authorities or law enforcement where required by applicable law

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable laws and our operational requirements.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Manage location permissions
  • Manage notification permissions
  • Contact us regarding privacy concerns

9. Children's Privacy

RucHiGo is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services

RucHiGo may use third-party services for functions such as payment processing, maps and location services, cloud infrastructure, analytics, authentication, notifications, and security.

These third parties may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, analyze usage, and maintain security.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements, or business practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us.
