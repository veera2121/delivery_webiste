Delete Your RucHiGO Account

You can request deletion of your RucHiGO customer account and associated personal data through this page.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a request from your registered email address, if available.
  2. Include your registered mobile number so we can identify your account.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Do not send passwords or OTPs by email. A deletion request will be processed after the account is appropriately verified.

What happens to your data?

Eligible account information and personal data will be deleted or anonymized after verification. Certain order, payment, tax, or other records may be retained when required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

Contact us

Email: info@ruchigo.in

We will communicate the outcome of your request and any applicable retention requirements.
